🏃 Running

4–5 runs per week, roughly 3–6 miles each. Most easy, a little hard, one long.

Easy aerobic (zone 2)

2–3 runs at conversational pace. Builds the engine and recovers well.
~75% of weekly volume

One longer run

Your longest run of the week, still easy. Add distance gradually.
Longest single run

One quality session

Tempo, intervals or strides. Short, focused, finished feeling strong.

Progression & recovery

  • Grow weekly volume ~10% max
  • Every 4th week: lighter
  • Sore or tired? Swap for a walk

🏋️ Strength

Back in the gym, 2–3 sessions per week. Keep lifting heavy to hold muscle while losing fat.

Compound lifts

  • Squat or leg press
  • Hinge (deadlift / RDL)
  • Press (bench / overhead)
  • Pull (rows / pull-ups)

Maintain during a cut

Keep the weight on the bar; reduce sets if recovery suffers. Strength holding steady = success.

Pair with running

Lift legs on easy-run days or after rest days — not before the quality session.

🥗 Nutrition

Moderate, sustainable, satisfying.

Moderate deficit

A small daily deficit you can keep for months beats a crash diet.

Protein-forward

Build every meal around a protein source, then add plants and fiber.

Smaller meals

Lighter, more even meals help avoid post-meal slumps.

Hydration

Water through the day, more on run days and in the heat.

Mindful alcohol

Fewer drinking days, set a limit in advance, alternate with water. It affects sleep, recovery and appetite.

📉 Body composition

Watch the trend, not the day.

7-day average

Weigh daily, same conditions; track the rolling weekly average.

Waist measurement

Measure every 2 weeks at the same spot — often more telling than the scale.

Ignore daily noise

Salt, water, and sleep swing daily readings. Smart-scale body-fat % is noisy too.

Maintenance breaks

Every 8–12 weeks, eat at maintenance for 1–2 weeks to reset.

😴 Sleep

Target 8–9 hours

Sleep is where training turns into fitness.

Consistent schedule

Same wake time daily, wind-down routine, dark cool room.

Track it

Use a tracker for trends, and check it’s still syncing.

❤️ Heart health

Home blood pressure

  • Sit quietly 5 minutes, feet flat, back supported
  • Arm at heart level, correct cuff size
  • No caffeine/exercise 30 min before
  • Take 2–3 readings, 1 min apart
  • Log them and share with your clinician

Cholesterol rechecks

Get periodic lipid panels on the schedule your doctor advises; track the trend over time.

Stand up slowly

Rise gradually, especially at night or after a hot shower, and stay hydrated. Report dizziness to your clinician.

🚀 Phases & milestones

Phase 1 · Rebuild

4–6 weeks. Re-establish routine, easy miles, relearn lifts, fix sleep.
Milestone: 4 runs + 2 lifts for 3 straight weeks.

Phase 2 · Cut

8–16 weeks. Moderate deficit, hold strength, add a quality run.
Milestone: steady downward trend, lifts maintained.

Phase 3 · Maintain

Ongoing. Eat at maintenance, keep the habits, set a new performance goal.
Milestone: a race, PR, or a strength goal.
